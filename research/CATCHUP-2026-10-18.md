## 2. What Draft Day has become vs the original @poteto bookmark intent

### 2.1 What the bookmarks asked for (Fri research)

From `research/POTETO-BOOKMARKS.md` and `research/BRIEF.md` §1, the borrow list was:

1. **Arena contract** — same prompt to N candidates, hidden rubric, cross-judge, pick a base, graft by hand, verify (`arena` skill).
2. **Verification as the gate** — not CI green alone (`create-verification-skill`, `principle-prove-it-works`, blast-radius ladder).
3. **Correction order of value** — architecture > lint/test > skill/rule > human review ("ngmi"), later productized as `/correct`.

### 2.3 Where Draft Day diverged (on purpose)

1. **Vocabulary is Boyd's, not Lauren's.** NFL draft terms (scouting, combine, film room, trade, physical, war room) instead of Michelin kitchen metaphors. Decision logged Fri in LOG.tsv (`architecture` / "Name: Draft Day").
2. **Engine outside the agent session.** pstack arena runs inside one Cursor chat. Draft Day externalizes state to an event log + `next(pick)` so Grok Bot can revive across hours and still know what to launch. That is the Fri synthesis decision A in `ARCHITECTURE.md` §4.
3. **Angles instead of model families (so far).** CloudAgent only sets a model when Boyd names one. Diversity came from named angles (e.g. P5: frame engine / broadcast booth / chapter cuts). Board is supposed to show the model each run actually used; per-role models remain an open Boyd question (`ARCHITECTURE.md` §7 Q3).
4. **Stacked draft PRs, not merge-ready trunk.** Until Boyd merges, every pick's PR base is the previous draft branch. Cost accepted in SPEC-004 and SPEC-005: later PRs cannot land before earlier ones.
5. **Box-side deterministic judges for the math.** `tools/filmroom-calc.mjs` and phone/replay oracles re-score judge markdown so Grok Bot's second read is not vibes. That is `principle-build-the-lever` and `principle-explain-the-number` made concrete, even though those leaf skills were not named in Fri BRIEF.

### 2.4 Net: fidelity score

Draft Day is a **faithful outer-loop of pstack**, not a fork of pstack. The skills stay in the installed plugin; Draft Day is the production engine that *uses* them as design law. Fri BRIEF said "Borrow: the arena contract, verification as the gate… Do not borrow: auto-merge." Sat–Sun execution stayed inside that fence.

## 5. Lessons learned from the Saturday arenas

These are the operational lessons worth encoding (Pick 3's job) or writing into Pick 6's linter. Sources: LOG.tsv Sat rows + film-room-result.md files.

### 5.1 W19 grafter paths (Pick 4 physical)

**What happened.** Physical flagged `docs/SPEC-005` and `test/phone.pooled.test.ts` as touch-map violations. Both were **orchestrator-ordered graft adds** (same pattern as PR #5). Journeys, probes, and safety facts already passed.

**Ruling.** Sanctioned graft paths → reply queue to physical for VERIFY-9-only re-check, **not** counted as a fix loop. Then VERIFIED, fixLoops 0.

**Lesson.** The touch map must distinguish (a) candidate freelancing vs (b) graft brief requirements. Encode as: grafter allowlist paths in the physical brief, or a `graft_artifacts` section the verifier reads before failing W19. Candidate for Pick 3 gardener finding → engine check, and for Pick 6 scouting linter ("does VERIFY mention graft-only paths?").

### 5.2 W8 / 44px targets (Pick 4)

**What happened.** c1 kept original desktop control sizes at 1280 (Add pick ~96×35). c2/c3 applied `min-height: 44px` more globally. Phone probes (390/430) passed for all; 1280 W8 failed only on c1. Judges scored thumb 4 for c1 because W8 acceptance was scoped to phone widths.

**Lesson.** Write viewport scope into the rubric and the probe matrix *before* launch ("W8 at ≤480 only" vs "all widths"). Otherwise the Film Room burns a trade slot on something that was never a phone bug. Encode in Pick 6: every criterion must name the viewport or surface it applies to.

### 5.3 Committed screenshots (Pick 5)

**What happened.** SPEC-006 required byte-identical fixtures and committed PNGs; graft PR #7 includes 10 PNGs; physical copied finals to `runs/p5/screens-final/`.

### 5.4 Judge swap-consistency (Picks 3–5)

| Pick | Pass 1 vs Pass 2 | Outcome |

**Lesson.** Max 2 fix loops (`ARCHITECTURE.md` budget) is enough so far. The expensive miss was a **boundary escape** (untrusted payload → JSON), exactly what SPEC-003/004 forbade. ### 5.6 Stacking vs siblings

P2 was a sibling of P1 (touch-mapped). P3 onward stacked on newest draft tip. Mode S on P4 even edited `lessons.css` so Locker Room inherited phone tokens.

**Lesson.** Sibling picks save calendar time but freeze an older type shape (SPEC-004 explains why P3 could not sibling off scaffold: `validateJudgementContext` only exists on the stack). Default: **stack**. Sibling only with an explicit touch map and a mergesim proof (P4 did mergesim in LOG).

### 5.7 Angle diversity worked without model diversity

Three angles per pick produced real architectural forks (registries vs pipelines vs UI-first; frame engine vs chapters). Judges repeatedly rejected "extra public surface" and "phone order that freezes a private layout."

**Lesson.** Until Boyd names per-role models, keep writing angles that force **whole-shape** alternatives (`architect` Phase B / exhaust-the-design-space), not point tweaks inside one shape.

### 5.8 Holding the next launch on physical

P4 launch was held while P3 fix loop ran, then re-grounded START `06ce1ae` → `e242a53` and re-checked oracles.

**Lesson.** Never launch the next combine on a NOT_VERIFIED tip. Re-ground oracles after every fix commit (phone-calc counts can change if UI CSS moved; P3's fix did not touch `src/ui`, so oracles stayed).
